Our Story

Crafting clean beauty with intention since 2018

The Beginning

Founded on a Simple Belief

ZINAIA was born from a personal journey. After years of struggling with sensitive skin and feeling frustrated by products filled with unnecessary chemicals, our founder set out to create something different.

We believe that skincare should be simple, effective, and transparent. Every ingredient we use has a purpose, and every product we create is designed to deliver real results without compromising your skin or the environment.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do.

Clean Formulas

We never use parabens, sulfates, phthalates, or artificial fragrances. Every ingredient serves a purpose.

Cruelty Free

We never test on animals. Period. Our products are certified cruelty-free by Leaping Bunny.

Sustainable

From recyclable packaging to responsibly sourced ingredients, we minimize our environmental footprint.

Science-Backed

Our formulas are developed with dermatologists and backed by clinical research for proven efficacy.
